Ideas for making change work.

Practical thinking on technology, transformation and the decisions that turn ambition into progress.

Artificial intelligence

Agentic AI needs a business case before a use case.

Agentic AI can attract attention for what it can do. A better starting point is what the business needs to achieve. Choose a real workflow, understand its current cost and constraints, and define the result that would make a change worthwhile.

From there, assess whether an AI-enabled approach is appropriate, what human oversight it needs, and how to measure its impact. That keeps experimentation connected to outcomes rather than activity.

Business transformation

A better starting point for transformation.

Large change programmes can begin with a solution before the problem is clearly framed. Start by aligning the people involved on what needs to improve, why it matters now, and how you will recognise progress.

A clear outcome gives teams a way to make trade-offs, sequence work and learn along the way. It also makes it easier to adjust the plan when new information appears.

Managed services

Managed services should create room to improve.

Reliable day-to-day support matters. So does making time to address recurring issues and improve how work gets done. A useful managed services relationship makes ownership clear, communicates consistently and creates a regular rhythm for reviewing priorities.

When expectations and measures are agreed up front, ongoing support can become a foundation for progress, not just a way to keep the lights on.
